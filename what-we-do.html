What We Do

Support for education, development, partnerships and impact.

01

Helping people navigate opportunity with clarity.

We support students, families and educational communities to make informed decisions about education, pathways and opportunities.

  • University and career guidance
  • Student application support
  • Educational pathway planning
  • Family information and engagement
  • Student development programmes
  • Educational workshops
  • University and institutional partnerships
  • Youth opportunity programmes
02

Helping educational organisations become stronger from within.

We support schools and educational organisations to strengthen the systems, practices and culture that enable people and students to thrive.

  • School culture development
  • Staff development and training
  • Student wellbeing systems
  • Safeguarding support
  • Policy and procedure development
  • Quality assurance
  • Organisational review
  • Leadership support
03

Turning ideas into organised action.

We support organisations to develop, coordinate and deliver purposeful educational and development initiatives.

  • Project design
  • Project coordination
  • Programme development
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Partnership development
  • Event and programme coordination
  • Educational initiatives
  • Youth programmes
04

Using evidence to understand what works.

We help organisations build stronger programmes by collecting, analysing and using evidence to understand progress and improve outcomes. This area can grow gradually as TILMO develops its capacity.

  • Monitoring and evaluation
  • Programme evaluation
  • Impact measurement
  • Data collection
  • Reporting
  • Learning and reflection
  • Programme improvement
